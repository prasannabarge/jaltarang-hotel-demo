Welcome to Tranquility

Serene Lakeside Hospitality in the Heart of Sahyadri Valley

Nestled directly along the calm waters of Lakefront Drive, Jaltarang Hotel & Lakeside Resort blends contemporary architectural warmth with timeless Indian hospitality. Designed for couples, families, and discerning weekend travelers, every room is oriented to capture crisp valley breezes, manicured tropical gardens, or uninterrupted lakefront sunsets.

Our signature dining venue, Tarang Kitchen & Grill, proudly operates completely separate kitchens, preparation counters, and utensils for Pure Veg and Non-Veg culinary traditions—giving every guest complete peace of mind alongside fresh regional Maharashtrian, North Indian, and coastal specialties.
